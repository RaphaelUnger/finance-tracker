Design Directions — Finance Tracker (6 options)

Minimal — Quick Add Focus
Rationale: Lowest friction. Large FAB and spacious list. Good for casual users.
Card-focused — Receipt Thumbs
Rationale: Emphasizes receipts and visual cues. Good for users keeping photo records.
Dashboard-first — Charts Upfront
Rationale: For users who check reports frequently. Quick glance summaries.
List-first Compact — Power Users
Rationale: Dense lists and swipe actions. Optimized for fast edits.
Visual-centric — Reports First
Rationale: Rich visual charts and timeline. Best for analytic users.
Compact Privacy-first — Subtle Security Cues
Rationale: Subtle privacy indicators, encryption status. Builds trust with visual cues.
